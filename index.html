⏳ Mission 3: Future grammar

Remember: going to = a plan already decided. will = a decision made at the time of speaking. Present simple = timetables and fixed plans.

GOING TO

We’re going to visit Salalah.

A plan decided before speaking.

WILL

I’ll take a small bag, too.

A decision made now.

PRESENT SIMPLE

The train leaves at 6 o’clock.

A timetable or fixed plan.
